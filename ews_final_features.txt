=====================================================================
EARLY WARNING SYSTEM (SENTINEL) - FINAL CLUBBED FEATURE SPEC
=====================================================================
Design: ~50 raw features compressed into 8 scenario modules, each with
final features + 1 composite index. Every feature has a plain-English
reason code. Three outputs per customer:
  (1) Warning Tier        : Green / Watch / Amber / Red
  (2) Evidence Confidence : High / Medium / Low
  (3) Trajectory          : Improving / Stable / Deteriorating / Recovering

Conventions
- Windows: 3m / 6m / 12m rolling, as-of the scoring date.
- norm(x) = percentile rank or min-max, clipped to [0,1].
- Missing data is stored as NULL, never as 0 (see Scenario 7).
- Weights are starting values; tune on synthetic ground-truth labels.

=====================================================================
SCENARIO 1: REPAYMENT BEHAVIOUR DETERIORATION
=====================================================================
Input per instalment: due_date, due_amount, paid_date, paid_amount,
DPD = paid_date - due_date.

F1.1 Lateness Rate            = late_payments / total_due (6m)
F1.2 Delinquency Severity     = 0.6*avg_DPD + 0.4*max_DPD (6m), cap 90
F1.3 Payment Shortfall Ratio  = 1 - (sum paid / sum due) (3m, 6m)
F1.4 Missed Instalment Intens.= missed_6m + 2*max_consecutive_missed
F1.5 Repayment Inconsistency  = std-dev of days-late (6m)
F1.6 Deterioration Trend      = slope of monthly DPD (6m) + (3m vs prior 3m)
F1.7 Baseline Shift Score     = (recent_3m_lateness - own_12m_mean)/own_12m_std
F1.8 Recurrence Flag (gate)   = 1 if late in >=3 of last 6 months
                                (also count delinquency episodes)

Composite: RBDI (0-100) =
  100 * (0.20*norm(F1.1) + 0.20*norm(F1.2) + 0.15*norm(F1.3)
       + 0.15*norm(F1.4) + 0.05*norm(F1.5) + 0.15*norm(F1.6)
       + 0.10*norm(F1.7))
Recurrence Flag is a gate, not part of the sum.

Rules
- Recurrence=0, low lateness, clean history  -> isolated delay, no escalation
- Recurrence=1 and Trend>0                   -> sustained, escalate
- Baseline Shift high but RBDI low           -> Watch (early shift)
- Shortfall high but Lateness low            -> silent partial payer, flag
- Consecutive missed >=2                     -> hard trigger, Amber or above
Edge cases: tenure<6m -> use available months, lower confidence; zero
variance -> z=0 and mark "insufficient baseline"; cap DPD at 90.

Reason codes
- "Paid late in 4 of last 6 instalments"
- "Paying only 70% of due amount on average"
- "Average delay rising ~3 days per month"
- "Behaviour is 2.4 sigma worse than own 12-month norm"

=====================================================================
SCENARIO 2: DECLINING INCOME AND CASH FLOW
=====================================================================
Input: monthly account inflows/outflows, salary credits (date, source),
closing/average daily balance, monthly EMI.

F2.1 Inflow Trend Slope       = regression slope of 6m monthly inflow
                                / mean inflow (% change per month)
F2.2 Decline Magnitude        = 1 - (3m avg inflow / 12m avg inflow)
F2.3 Consecutive Decline Count= months in a row inflow < prior month
                                (use a small tolerance, e.g. >2% drop);
                                flag when >=3
F2.4 Inflow Volatility        = coefficient of variation (6m)
F2.5 Income Stability Index   = club of salary regularity (std-dev of
                                credit day, missed salary cycles) and
                                source concentration (HHI of credit
                                sources); lower = less stable
F2.6 Net Cash Flow Ratio      = (inflow - outflow)/inflow, 3m avg + trend
F2.7 EMI Coverage Buffer      = closing balance / (monthly EMI / 30) in
                                days; also inflow / EMI coverage ratio

Composite: CFSI (Cash Flow Stress Index, 0-100) =
  100 * (0.25*norm(F2.1) + 0.20*norm(F2.2) + 0.20*norm(F2.3)
       + 0.10*norm(F2.4) + 0.10*norm(F2.5) + 0.10*norm(F2.6)
       + 0.05*norm(F2.7 inverted))

Key rule ("Silent Stress"): CFSI high AND repayments current (RBDI low)
-> flag Watch/Amber. DEMO THIS CASE.

Reason codes
- "Monthly inflow fell 3 months in a row (-22% vs 12m average)"
- "Balance covers only 9 days of EMI"
- "Salary credits arriving irregularly"

=====================================================================
SCENARIO 3: INCREASING OBLIGATIONS AND EXPOSURE
=====================================================================
Input: facilities (limits, outstanding), EMIs, enquiries, income.

F3.1 Utilisation Level + Trend = current utilisation, 3m change in pp
F3.2 New Credit Velocity       = 2*new_facilities_90d + enquiries_90d
F3.3 DSR Level + Trend         = total EMIs / income, and 3m/6m change
F3.4 Exposure Growth Rate      = % growth in total outstanding (6m)
F3.5 Income Growth Rate        = % growth in income/inflow (6m)
F3.6 Exposure-Capacity Gap     = (1 + F3.4) / (1 + F3.5)
                                 (>1 means exposure growing faster than
                                 ability to repay) KEY METRIC
F3.7 Headroom Ratio            = available limit / total limit; also
                                 headroom left after EMIs

Composite: LPI (Leverage Pressure Index, 0-100) =
  100 * (0.25*norm(F3.6) + 0.25*norm(F3.3) + 0.20*norm(F3.1)
       + 0.15*norm(F3.2) + 0.15*norm(F3.7 inverted))

Reason codes
- "Exposure up 35% while income up 4% in 6 months"
- "DSR rose from 38% to 52%"
- "2 new facilities and 5 enquiries in last 90 days"

=====================================================================
SCENARIO 4: TEMPORARY MISSED PAYMENTS (EXCEPTION ENGINE)
=====================================================================
Triggers only when a missed/late event is found. Produces a Mitigation
Score that can downgrade the tier by one level.

F4.1 Calendar Exemption Flag   = due date on bank holiday/weekend or
                                 payment within 2 working days after
F4.2 Salary Delay Flag         = salary credit arrived late in the same
                                 cycle and payment followed within X days
F4.3 Recovery Speed            = days to clear arrears; 1 if next cycle
                                 paid on time and arrears cleared
F4.4 Clean History Depth       = consecutive on-time payments before the
                                 event (e.g. 24) and prior late count
F4.5 Evidence Flag             = documented processing/system failure or
                                 customer-communication note
F4.6 Event Isolation           = only 1 miss in last 12m

Mitigation Score (0-100) =
  100 * (0.20*F4.1 + 0.20*F4.2 + 0.20*norm(F4.3)
       + 0.20*norm(F4.4) + 0.10*F4.5 + 0.10*F4.6)
If score >= threshold -> mitigated flag, tier downgraded one level,
reason shown.

Guardrails (never mitigate if)
- consecutive missed >=2, or Recurrence Flag = 1
- CFSI or LPI is high (underlying stress present)

Example output: "Missed payment on 26 Jan (Republic Day), paid within
2 days, 24 prior on-time payments -> no escalation."

=====================================================================
SCENARIO 5: EARLY DELINQUENCY AND ROLL-FORWARD RISK
=====================================================================
Model: DPD bucket transition (roll-rate / Markov) matrix, estimated by
segment (product/vintage) and adjusted per customer with features.
States: Current -> 1-30 -> 31-60 -> 61-90 -> NPA (90+).

F5.1 Current DPD Bucket (state)
F5.2 Roll-Forward Probability  = P(move to next worse bucket) at 30d, 60d
F5.3 Self-Cure Probability     = P(return to Current)
F5.4 Delinquency Episode Count = episodes in last 12m
F5.5 Avg Cure Time             = mean days to cure in past episodes
F5.6 Worsening Flag            = this episode peak > previous peak, or
                                 DPD rising across consecutive cycles
F5.7 Exposure at Risk          = outstanding balance (Rs)
Inputs borrowed from other modules: CFSI (income trend), LPI (exposure).

Composite: Roll Risk Score = calibrated probability (ML or Markov
adjusted) of rolling to a worse bucket within 30/60 days.

Recovery Profile label
- Quick-Recover : first episode, fast prior cures, CFSI low
- Repeat        : >=2 episodes, each cured
- Worsening     : Worsening Flag = 1 or cure times lengthening

Example output: "68% probability to roll into 31-60 DPD within 30 days."

=====================================================================
SCENARIO 6: SME AND BUSINESS FINANCIAL STRESS
=====================================================================
Input: business account transactions, ODs, cheque/ECS returns,
GST-like turnover (synthetic), supplier payments, payer details.

Seasonality adjustment (applied first): deseasonalise receipts with an
industry seasonal index (month avg / yearly avg, or YoY comparison) so
seasonal businesses are NOT falsely flagged.

F6.1 Receipts Trend            = slope + 3m vs 12m change of
                                 deseasonalised receipts
F6.2 Receipt-to-EMI Coverage   = monthly receipts / EMI (trend + level)
F6.3 Liquidity Trend           = club of operating balance and ADB:
                                 ADB slope, minimum balance, days below
                                 threshold
F6.4 Bounce Intensity          = weighted cheque/ECS bounces (90d)
F6.5 Activity Irregularity     = txn count change, max gap in days,
                                 active-days ratio
F6.6 Working-Capital Strain    = club of overdraft utilisation trend and
                                 supplier-payment stretching (payment
                                 delay trend)
F6.7 Turnover Divergence       = GST-like turnover vs bank receipts
                                 mismatch
F6.8 Payer Concentration       = top-3 payer share (or HHI)

Composite: SME Stress Index (0-100) =
  100 * (0.25*norm(F6.1) + 0.15*norm(F6.2 inverted)
       + 0.15*norm(F6.3) + 0.10*norm(F6.4) + 0.10*norm(F6.5)
       + 0.10*norm(F6.6) + 0.05*norm(F6.7) + 0.10*norm(F6.8))

Reason codes
- "Seasonally adjusted receipts down 3 months in a row"
- "Receipts cover EMI only 1.1x (was 2.4x)"
- "2 ECS bounces in last 60 days"

=====================================================================
SCENARIO 7: MISSING OR INCOMPLETE HISTORY
=====================================================================
Separates "no evidence" from "bad evidence".

F7.1 History Length Score      = min(months_on_book / 12, 1)
F7.2 Transaction Coverage      = months with data / expected months
                                 (and active-days coverage)
F7.3 Bureau Availability       = 1 if bureau record present, else 0
F7.4 Field Completeness        = share of non-null key fields

Composite: Data Completeness Score (DCS, 0-100) =
  100 * (0.35*F7.1 + 0.30*F7.2 + 0.15*F7.3 + 0.20*F7.4)
Evidence Confidence: High >=75, Medium 40-75, Low <40.

Rules
- Missing features stay NULL; impute from peer-group/segment baseline
  and widen the score uncertainty band by (1 - DCS).
- If DCS < 40 and no hard trigger -> tier capped at "Watch -
  Insufficient Evidence" (never Red from missing data alone).
- Hard evidence (e.g. DPD >30, consecutive missed >=2) still escalates.
- Recommended action: "collect more data / light-touch check".

Demo: new customer with no history -> "Monitor, low confidence", not
"High Risk".

=====================================================================
SCENARIO 8: PORTFOLIO CONCENTRATION AND RISK CLUSTERS
=====================================================================
Segments: product, industry, region, branch, vintage, channel (and
combinations).

F8.1 Segment Deterioration Rate = % of customers whose tier worsened
                                  or composite index rose (30/90d)
F8.2 Segment Lift / Z-score     = segment rate vs portfolio rate
F8.3 Exposure at Risk (Rs)      = outstanding of Amber/Red customers per
                                  segment, plus HHI of at-risk exposure
F8.4 Spread Breadth             = % of segment customers showing the
                                  signal; number of distinct signal types
F8.5 Momentum                   = week-over-week change in F8.1
F8.6 Cluster Membership         = unsupervised label (e.g. HDBSCAN on
                                  behaviour features) + cluster size
F8.7 Isolation Test             = share of segment at-risk exposure from
                                  top-3 customers (high = isolated,
                                  low = broad)

Composite: Cluster Alert Score =
  norm(F8.2) * norm(F8.3) * (1 + max(F8.5, 0))
Guardrail: minimum segment size (e.g. >=30 customers) to avoid
small-sample false alarms.

Drill-down: segment -> customers -> drivers (reason codes).

=====================================================================
FINAL TIER ASSIGNMENT (HOW MODULES COMBINE)
=====================================================================
Per customer:
  Risk Signal = weighted blend of RBDI, CFSI, LPI, Roll Risk Score
                (+ SME Stress Index for business customers)
  ML layer    = 30/60/90-day deterioration probability using the final
                features above as inputs
  Exceptions  = Scenario 4 can downgrade one tier (with guardrails)
  Confidence  = Scenario 7 DCS sets Evidence Confidence and caps tier
  Portfolio   = Scenario 8 adds a "part of emerging cluster" tag, may
                raise priority but not the individual tier
  Priority    = expected loss (PD x LGD x EAD) x urgency, to control
                alert fatigue

Tier mapping (starting thresholds, tune on labels)
  Green : blended risk < 25
  Watch : 25-45  (or low-confidence capped)
  Amber : 45-70
  Red   : > 70, or hard trigger

Hard triggers (override): consecutive missed >=2, DPD >30,
Roll Risk (30d) > 70% with Worsening Flag.

Output record per customer
  customer_id, tier, risk_score, confidence, trajectory,
  top 3-5 reason codes, mitigated flag + reason, roll probabilities,
  recovery profile, cluster tag, recommended action, persona

=====================================================================
MINIMUM DATA SCHEMA
=====================================================================
customers   : id, segment (retail/SME), product, industry, region,
              branch, channel, vintage, income
repayments  : customer_id, facility_id, due_date, due_amount,
              paid_date, paid_amount
transactions: customer_id, date, amount, direction, category/source,
              balance, bounce_flag
facilities  : customer_id, facility_id, limit, outstanding, emi,
              open_date
enquiries   : customer_id, date
sme_extra   : customer_id, month, gst_turnover, supplier_payments,
              od_utilisation
calendar    : date, bank_holiday_flag
events      : customer_id, date, type (system failure, note)
labels      : customer_id, deteriorated_30/60/90 (ground truth)
